The Rules

You must use at least 🥑🥑🥑🥑🥑🥑🥑🥑 (8) avocados

Bring something! Guacamole, beer or friends. We’ll provide the chips (they're from Nopalito!), the prizes, and of course… the coveted Guacamole Glory Trophy.

Judging

You and your neighbors are the judges when it comes to this competition, and you’ll be asked to vote with your eyes, your heart, and of course, your taste buds.

The winner to be crowned with the Guacamole Glory Trophy, bragging rights until next summer, the title of “San Francisco’s Official Guacamole” and a secret mystery prize!
